AquaTrace helps you keep track of what and how much you drink each day, with statistics for hydration, caffeine, alcohol, and nutrients.

## Contact Us

## FAQ

### Will I lose my data when I switch to a new device?

No. As long as your new device is signed in to the same iCloud account, your data syncs back automatically after you install AquaTrace.

### I'm not getting reminders to drink water.

Make sure notifications are allowed in your device's Settings → Notifications → AquaTrace, then check the reminder schedule in the App's settings.

### My records don't appear in Apple Health.

In Apple Health, tap your profile picture in the top-right corner, go to Apps under Privacy, find AquaTrace, and make sure writing is allowed.

### How do I delete my data?

See [Retention and Deletion](privacy/#4-retention-and-deletion) in the Privacy Policy.

### Can I use the numbers in the App as medical advice?

No. AquaTrace is a personal tracking tool, and its numbers are estimates based on your records. See the Disclaimer in the App for details.
